Cost register · current schedules only

Operating costs and future values

Every property-specific amount is intentionally blank. The public rates below are official reference schedules—not either home’s bill, a quote, or a forecast. No parcel, account, rent, value, or mortgage inputs are stored on this site.

Blank means “not verified,” not zero. The only responsible way to price a property is to match the correct parcel, billing period, meter, rate plan, lease, and ownership facts. Do not replace missing evidence with a generic percentage or a neighboring property’s numbers.

When evidence is ready

Two blank formulas for a future, sourced comparison

Annual property cash flow

Collected rent − vacancy / nonpayment − property tax − insurance − owner-paid utilities − repairs − capital reserve − management / compliance − debt service

Keep income tax and depreciation analysis separate. San Francisco rent rules may constrain collectible rent and occupancy. SF Rent Board guide.

After-tax sale proceeds

Sale price − mortgage payoff − transfer tax / recording costs − seller expenses − tax on gain

Use the rate in effect on the sale date and a reviewed tax basis. Main-home exclusions require their own ownership/use and other tests. SF transfer tax · IRS Pub. 523.
