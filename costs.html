Property tax, bonds, parcel charges Not published The bill generally applies the local rate to taxable assessed value, plus voter-approved bonded debt and parcel/direct assessments. A change in ownership or new construction can establish a new base-year value. The factored base-year value is generally capped at annual growth of the lower of California CPI or 2%, subject to exceptions and temporary decline-in-value rules; this is not a guaranteed 2% increase. Current covered-unit rent increase 1.6% · conditional The published annual allowable increase is 1.6% from March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027 for units covered by that local ordinance. Qualifying transfers by death or gift are exempt from California Documentary Transfer Tax under state law. Obtain a current title/escrow estimate and confirm local tax and filing forms before recording or sale. A transfer-tax exemption is not an income-tax or property-tax exemption. Separate cash and tax accounting

Keep these three measures distinct

Owner-occupied

Annual household carrying cost

tax + insurance + utilities + repairs + debt service + other cash costs

For a fair comparison, subtract the cost of alternative housing actually avoided. A property-tax exclusion changes only one part of the calculation.

BOE · property-tax framework
Rented

Cash flow before income tax

rent collected − vacancy − operating costs − debt service

Include owner-paid utilities, management, tenant turnover, insurance, assessments, and capital work. Do not subtract only the tax-deductible amount from cash rent.

IRS · rental accounting
Sold

Investable proceeds

sale price − selling costs − payoff − estimated sale taxes

Use an appraisal-backed basis and an actual tax review. An investment projection requires an explicit holding period, fee and tax assumptions, and risk tolerance.
