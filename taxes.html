Federal · California · property tax

Separate the estate, income, and property-tax questions.

“Inheritance tax,” federal estate tax, income tax on rents or a sale, property-tax reassessment, and documentary transfer tax are different systems with different taxpayers, filing rules, and deadlines.

Federal estate tax

The threshold is estate-level, not per beneficiary

i

There is no single beneficiary “inheritance allowance.”

For a U.S. citizen or resident, the Form 706 filing test compares the decedent's gross estate—potentially including real estate, cash, securities, insurance, certain trust interests, and other assets—plus adjusted taxable gifts and the specific gift-tax exemption, with the threshold for the year of death. A return can also be needed to elect portability even when the estate is below the ordinary filing threshold. The estate's filing or tax liability is not the same as a beneficiary's income tax.

!

A trust is a transfer tool, not a blanket tax exemption.

A properly funded living trust can help assets pass outside probate. It does not automatically eliminate the decedent's federal gross-estate inclusion, income tax on later rents or gains, or California property-tax reassessment. The result depends on the trust terms, ownership, powers retained, beneficiary, and actual use.
