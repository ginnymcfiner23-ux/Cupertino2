RESEARCH FLAG · MULTI-UNIT PROPERTY

“Same parcel” does not always mean “same home.”

BOE’s 2026 guidance says each dwelling in a multi-family property is considered separately and must independently qualify. In an apartment-complex example, only the unit the transferor occupied as a principal residence qualifies; the other units are reassessed at full cash value. A separate ADU/JADU example has different conditions.

If an intended transferee would occupy another unit, map the title, parcel/unit assessments, prior homeowner exemptions, and actual occupancy before relying on an exclusion.

A narrow 2027 change—not a general expansion.
