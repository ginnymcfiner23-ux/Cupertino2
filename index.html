First decision gate

Check Prop 19 before renting or selling.

  • Eligibility depends on the qualifying family relationship and whether the transferor's home met the principal-residence test.
  • At least one eligible child or grandchild generally must occupy the home as a principal residence within one year and timely claim the homeowners' or disabled-veterans' exemption.
  • The current indexed allowance is $1,044,586 for transfers from February 16, 2025 through February 15, 2027. The next amount is not yet published as of this review.

A rental or second home is not automatically eligible. Multi-unit and partial-interest transfers have additional rules; occupancy and continued qualification matter.
