Current law is not a 2040 forecast

Show what is known. Leave the rest blank.

No official source supports parcel-specific prices, rents, taxes, insurance, utilities, repairs, or portfolio returns for the 2040–2050 horizon. We publish the latest verified rule or scheduled amount, then let a private user model only verified inputs and explicit assumptions.

!

There is no numeric winner or reliable property projection here.

Long-range estimates would require forecasts that are not officially supportable, plus private facts that must not be published. Do not mistake a current exclusion limit, rent-board percentage, assessment rule, or sample investment return for a forecast of a future transfer.

i

Privacy and limits

Inputs stay in this browser page; this worksheet has no storage, analytics, or network submission. It cannot determine tax, Prop 19 eligibility, rent legality, fair market value, insurance, or investment suitability. It excludes income-tax effects except a sale-tax estimate you enter, and it does not inflate costs or model Home B's future value.
