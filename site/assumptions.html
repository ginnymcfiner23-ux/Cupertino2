Which assumptions matter most

  1. Portfolio return (7%). This is the single most influential assumption in the whole model, because it sets the opportunity cost of the equity. The sensitivity is displayed on the Scenarios page: the break-even is around 0.68%, so the conclusion survives almost any reasonable value.
  2. Whether the house is the transferor's principal residence. Not really an assumption — an unknown fact that must be established. See FLAG-05.
  3. Maintenance and insurance. Together these are the difference between the house roughly paying for itself and it bleeding cash. They are also the least reliable numbers in the model.
  4. Market rent. Affects both A and B equally, so it does not change their ranking, but it does change their distance from C.

The two market values are also requester estimates. Confirm with an appraisal.
