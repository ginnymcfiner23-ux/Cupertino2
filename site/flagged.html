FLAG-13 low Estate size is unknown, so whether the federal estate tax applies cannot be determined The request states the inheritance includes 'a considerable amount of money' that 'could be over' the exempt amount. The federal basic exclusion is $15,000,000 per person for 2026 ($30,000,000 for a married couple with a timely portability election), made permanent by OBBBA and indexed for inflation from 2027. California and its local governments impose no estate or inheritance tax at all. No estate tax is owed unless the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts exceeds the exclusion for the year of death. Size the gross estate (real property at date-of-death FMV plus all other assets, plus adjusted taxable gifts) against the exclusion for the actual year of death. Note that the $15M is per person, and that portability requires a timely Form 706. Internal Revenue Serviceofficial

Values deliberately left blank

Per the verification rule, no value has been inferred where an official source could not be found. Specifically:

  • Rent for the third unit at 94122. The supplied $7,150/month covers two units. Rather than divide by two, the model is constructed so that this figure cancels out of every comparison between the options. It is not needed.
  • Cost basis of either property. Not supplied. It is not needed for the inheritance analysis, because IRC § 1014 resets the basis at death. It would be needed to price a lifetime sale precisely, which is why the sell-now comparison is presented as a marginal tax rate rather than a dollar figure.
  • Parcel-specific tax rate. Not published per parcel. The model derives it from the supplied bill history and discloses the derivation.
  • Insurance premium. No official filing publishes a ZIP-level average. Flagged rather than filled in.
