Projections

Assessed value, market value and property tax out to 2055, assuming no change in ownership.

Projections assume no change in ownership: the assessed value grows at the Proposition 13 inflation factor of 2.0% a year, and the market value grows at the stated rate. Property tax is the parcel bill model applied to the projected assessed value, with flat charges inflating at 2.5% a year.

What happens at the transfer

These projections stop being valid the moment the trust distributes the property. See Proposition 19 for the reassessment that follows, which replaces the assessed value with either the market value minus the cap, or the full market value.

Proposition 8 is also worth knowing about: if the market value on 1 January falls below the factored base year value, the assessor enrols the lower figure, and it can then rise by more than 2% a year on the way back up (but never above the factored base year value).
