Recommendation (base case)

Property A (Cupertino): Sell at inheritance is the higher-value option in the base case (inheritance 2045, 10-year horizon). Selling beats living there in 10 of 10 sensitivity cases. Living there becomes worthwhile only if the heir would otherwise pay rent above about $204,245 per year (year-of-inheritance dollars).

These results depend on assumptions that are not sourced (appreciation, return, maintenance, selling cost). Change them and test the sensitivity table on the Projections page before acting.

Key tax facts

  • Transfers by death, including distributions from a trust, are covered by Prop 19 (BOE LTA 2026/026).
  • Prop 19 excludes the family home only when the heir makes it their principal residence. Rental property is not eligible (BOE).
  • On a sale soon after death, the basis is generally the value at death, so little gain is expected (IRS Pub 551; confirm).
  • No documentary transfer tax applies to transfers by reason of death (RTC §11930).
  • Federal estate exclusion: $15M per person for 2026 deaths (IRS).
