Inheritance steps (California trust)

A general sequence for a trust-held inheritance of two California properties. This is research, not legal advice. Have an estate attorney or CPA confirm each step against the trust documents.

StepWhat to doSource (official)
1Confirm the trust terms and the successor trustee
Read the trust for distribution rules and whether the property goes outright or in continuing trust. Prop 19 applies to distributions from a trust.		BOE-LTA-2026-026
2Obtain date-of-death values for both properties
Appraisal or broker opinion of value at the date of death. This sets the new basis for a later sale (step-up).		IRS-PUB-551
3Check federal estate tax filing
Form 706 is required when the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts exceeds the filing threshold for the year of death ($15,000,000 for 2026). Portability to a surviving spouse requires a timely return.		IRS-ESTATE-TAX
4Deed from trustee to heir and documentary transfer tax exemption
Transfers by reason of death, outright or in trust, are exempt from the documentary transfer tax. Claim the exemption on the recorded deed.		RTC-11930
5File the Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (BOE-502-A)
Required on the change in ownership. Mark the inheritance or parent-child box, as applicable.		BOE-502A
6Property A: choose occupancy and claim Prop 19 if eligible
Prop 19 keeps the value below the cap only if the heir makes it their principal residence and claims the homeowners' exemption within the BOE windows. Otherwise full reassessment applies.		BOE-LTA-2026-026 · BOE-PROP19-PAGE
7Property B: expect reassessment and a supplemental bill
Rental property is not eligible for the family-home exclusion. Expect a supplemental assessment, prorated from the change date. Confirm the mixed-use treatment with the Assessor.		BOE-PROP19-PAGE · SF-ASSESSOR · SF-TREASURER-SECURED
8Set up tax bills and due dates
SF secured taxes: first installment delinquent after Dec 10 (10% penalty); second after Apr 10 (10% plus fees).		SF-TREASURER-SECURED
9Property B: rental obligations
Owner must pay the Rent Board fee and keep the Housing Inventory current. The allowable increase for covered units is 1.6% for Mar 1, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027.		SF-RENT-BOARD
10Transfer utilities and insurance
Move water, electricity, and the property policy into the heir's name. Get a landlord policy for Property B.		SJW-AL-629A · SFPUC-RATES-2026-27 · CDI-HOME
11Decide: live, rent, or sell (see Decision page)
Compare the break-even rent for Property A and the keep vs sell results for Property B using the owner's own inputs.		BOE-LTA-2026-026
12If selling, track basis and holding period
Basis is the value at death. A sale soon after death generally produces little gain. Use the federal brackets for any gain.		IRS-REV-PROC-2025-32 · IRS-PUB-551
13Keep records
Retain the appraisal, the deed, the PCOR, Prop 19 filings, and tax bills. These support basis and exclusion claims.		IRS-PUB-551

Probate and trust-administration timelines vary. Court and trustee steps are not listed in detail here.
