Privacy boundary

What the public site deliberately leaves out

No direct property identifiers

No exact address, ZIP / postal code, parcel number, owner or beneficiary name, account number, or deed reference is published.

No private financial inputs

No property value, assessed value, rent, tax bill amount, mortgage, insurance premium, utility bill, maintenance estimate, or solar credit is copied into the public dashboard.

The city-level labels in this dashboard are used only to select relevant public law and tariffs. They do not establish a property’s exact location, assessor, service provider, or eligibility.

Close-out rule: A flag should be marked resolved only after a dated official bill, recorded document, signed lease, insurer quote, inspection, or other suitable evidence has been reviewed. Do not convert an unverified estimate into a “fact” just because it appears in an old worksheet.
