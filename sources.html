Sources and review notes

The dashboard’s factual statements are linked to the agencies, statutes, or published tariff documents below. Sources were reviewed on 6 November 2026; some pages are updated without notice. Re-open the source before relying on any deadline, rate, bill, or bill-status summary.

Evidence standard: search snippets were not treated as final authority. The research reviewed official pages, bill-status pages, statutes, guidance PDFs, current rate schedules, or linked government records. Exact property figures remain unverified and are not included in this public source register.

California property tax and title

Assessment, inherited property, and deadlines

Proposition 19 and Proposition 13

  • California Board of Equalization, Proposition 19. Current overview of eligible family transfers and home-use requirements. Open BOE Prop 19
  • BOE Legal Analysis 26-026. Detailed guidance on ownership, trust look-through, occupancy, claims, and related deadlines. Open LTA 26-026 PDF
  • BOE 2025 adjustment notice. States the $1,044,586 exclusion amount for eligible transfers from 16 February 2025 through 15 February 2027. This amount is not projected to a later period. Open BOE notice
  • BOE Publication 800-10. Prop 13 assessed-value basics, annual inflation factor, and reassessment context. Open BOE Pub. 800-10 PDF
  • BOE Publication 800-9. Death-related change-in-ownership reporting, including BOE-502-D timing. Open BOE Pub. 800-9 PDF

Trust administration and county property records

  • Santa Clara Superior Court, Probate Trusts. Local court guidance on trust administration and proceedings. Open Probate Trusts page
  • California Probate Code §16061.7. Statutory trust-notice requirements and timing. Open the statute
  • Santa Clara County Assessor, property records. Explains public assessed-value and parcel-record lookup; do not publish property identifiers. Open Assessor records page
  • San Francisco Assessor-Recorder. Official property-assessment, change-in-ownership, and recorded-document information. Open SF Assessor-Recorder
  • Santa Clara County Controller-Treasurer, rate-book archive. Tax rates by tax-rate area and fiscal year. The page reviewed listed FY 2025–26 and prior books; an FY 2026–27 rate book was not linked there. Open rate-book page
  • Santa Clara County Department of Tax and Collections. Current secured-property-tax information and bill lookup. Open DTAC · Open secured-tax lookup

Federal and California income / estate tax

Filing thresholds, inherited basis, and sale

Estate tax and basis

  • Internal Revenue Service, Estate Tax. Annual estate-tax filing threshold information; the 2026 threshold is not a future forecast. Open IRS Estate Tax
  • IRS Instructions for Form 706. Federal estate-tax return filing, deadline, portability, and related instructions. Open Form 706 instructions
  • California State Controller, Estate Tax. California filing status for deaths on or after 1 January 2005. Open State Controller page
  • IRS Publication 551. Basis of assets, including inherited property, subject to exceptions. Open IRS Pub. 551 PDF
  • IRS Publication 559. Survivors, executors, and administrators; fiduciary and final-return guidance. Open IRS Pub. 559
  • IRS Publication 523. Selling a home, ownership/use requirements, and potential exclusion. Open IRS Pub. 523
  • California Franchise Tax Board, sale of a home. California income-tax treatment and reporting. Open FTB home-sale guidance

Local tax, rental law, and utility schedules

Current references—not property-specific calculations

San Francisco and Santa Clara County

  • PG&E, rate advisories. Dated electricity and gas rate changes. Open PG&E advisories
  • California Public Utilities Commission, customer generation. Net Energy Metering and Net Billing Tariff overview. Open CPUC customer-generation page

  • California Contractors State License Board. License verification before hiring a contractor. Open CSLB license check
