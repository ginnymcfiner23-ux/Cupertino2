Worked example — Cupertino water from the official San Jose Water Company tariff

San Jose Water Company serves Cupertino (ZIP 95014). Its CPUC-approved Schedule 1-C residential tariff (effective 7/1/2026, Advice Letter 629A) charges a monthly service charge plus tiered quantity rates per Ccf (1 Ccf = 748 gallons):

Charge elementRate (eff. 7/1/2026)Source
Service charge, 5/8 x 3/4-inch meter$79.67/monthSJWC Schedule 1-C (AL 629A)
Quantity, 0–6 Ccf/month$5.0355/CcfSJWC Schedule 1-C (AL 629A)
Quantity, 6+–12 Ccf/month$7.5156/CcfSJWC Schedule 1-C (AL 629A)
Quantity, over 12 Ccf/month$14.3548/CcfSJWC Schedule 1-C (AL 629A)

Illustrative computation (not an official bill): at 10 Ccf/month — $79.67 + (6 × $5.0355) + (4 × $7.5156) ≈ $139.94/month, before any surcharges (e.g., the pending $0.2996/Ccf Advice Letter 627A surcharge would add ~$3.00). This brackets the owner's $100–$150/month estimate. Surcharges and CPUC rate cases change over time.

San Francisco water & sewer (SFPUC) — official rate schedule context

SFPUC FY 2026–27 rates (effective 7/1/2026): single-family water $12.20/unit for the first 4 units/month then $13.55/unit; multi-family $12.03/unit for the first 3 units per dwelling then $12.91/unit; monthly wastewater service charge $6.62; stormwater by parcel size tier ($12.63–$29.55/month depending on tier). SFPUC reports a typical single-family combined water+sewer bill of about $171–$189/month rising ~12.5%–12.6% per year under the proposed schedule.

Why this matters to the decision: holding both homes means paying property tax, insurance, water, and maintenance on two properties, while the Cupertino home's low Prop 13 base disappears on transfer (full reassessment unless the heir moves in and qualifies for the Prop 19 exclusion).
