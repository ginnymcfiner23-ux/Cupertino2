Law Changes Under Discussion & Recently Enacted

Laws change between now and the expected inheritance (2040–2050). This page tracks what has changed recently and what is under discussion, so the plan can be revisited before the transfer.

Recently enacted — verified

ChangeWhat it doesSource
Public Law 119-21 ("One Big Beautiful Bill Act," signed July 4, 2025) Set the federal estate/gift/GST basic exclusion at $15,000,000 per person for 2026, with no scheduled sunset, indexed for inflation after 2026. This canceled the prior Tax Cuts and Jobs Act sunset that would have cut the exclusion to roughly $7 million in 2026. IRS Newsroom · IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 · IRS Form 706 instructions
Proposition 19 value cap adjustment (Cal. Const. art. XIII A §2.1(c); RTC §63.2) The $1 million parent-child transfer value cap is adjusted every two years (February 16 of odd years) by the BOE using the California House Price Index: $1,000,000 (2021–2023), $1,022,600 (2023–2025), $1,044,586 (2/16/2025–2/15/2027). Next adjustment: February 2027. BOE Letter to Assessors 2025/009 · BOE Proposition 19 page

Under discussion or possible — not enacted (flagged)

ItemStatus & potential effectSource / flag
Future federal estate-tax legislation Congress could raise, lower, or repeal the federal estate tax at any time. No change enacted as of this writing; the $15,000,000 exclusion has no sunset but is not guaranteed. UNVERIFIED — monitor Congress / IRS
California changes to Proposition 13 or Proposition 19 No amendment modifying Prop 13 reassessment or Prop 19 intergenerational rules has been verified as enacted. Future ballot measures or legislation could alter the inheritance property-tax math significantly. UNVERIFIED — monitor BOE (boe.ca.gov/prop19/) and CA leginfo
California insurance market (FAIR Plan, wildfire exposure) Homeowners insurance availability and pricing in California are volatile; premiums could rise faster than projected, especially in higher-risk areas. UNVERIFIED — see California Dept. of Insurance
Property-tax rate changes (local bonds/assessments) Voter-approved bonds and special assessments are added on top of the 1% Prop 13 base and can change the effective rate without any state law change. BOE Pub 29 (rule is official; future rates are not)

Monitoring checklist before the transfer (~2040–2050)

  • Re-check the federal basic exclusion amount for the year of death (IRS Rev. Proc. inflation adjustments, published each fall).
  • Re-check the BOE Prop 19 value cap in effect at the transfer date (adjusted every February 16 of odd years).
  • Confirm whether any California ballot measure or statute has changed Prop 13/19 rules since this page was written.
  • Obtain current homeowners/landlord insurance quotes for both ZIP codes at decision time.
  • Confirm both parcels' assessed values with the county assessors before assuming any tax figure.
